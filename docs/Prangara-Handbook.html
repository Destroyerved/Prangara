4. Complete Page-by-Page Walkthrough

1. Workspace Hub

/workspace

What it is: Control center for managing one or multiple factories under your organization.

Features: Multi-plant comparison cards, machinery registries (boilers, motors, chillers), intake history, and auditable data quality scores.

How to use: Register new plant locations, switch your active factory, or drill down into specific equipment logs.

2. Account & Roles

/account

What it is: User identity, security settings, and organization permissions.

Features: Role-Based Access (Factory Owner, Plant Engineer, Auditor, Vendor), company switcher, and API tokens for ERP integration.

How to use: Invite team members with role-specific access, manage personal profile, and configure webhooks.

3. Notifications

/notifications

What it is: Factory early-warning and operational inbox.

Features: Sudden energy spike warnings (e.g. boiler fuel up 14%), incoming quotes from solar contractors, and CBAM deadline alerts.

How to use: Check pending alerts daily and click on an item to jump straight to the offending machine or bid.

4. Overview Dashboard

/overview

What it is: Single-page executive summary for the CEO and board.

Features: "Cash-Positive Opportunity" hero banner (₹ savings/yr), Scope 1/2/3 summary cards, and regional peer benchmark standing.

How to use: Review your headline emissions, potential rupee profit from greening, and top 3 priority actions.

5. Plant Data Intake

/assessment

What it is: Guided portal to input your factory's numbers with zero headache.

Features: 8-step wizard, local AI bill/nameplate camera reader, spreadsheet bulk uploader, and evidence attachment.

How to use: Upload monthly electricity bills or fuel sheets; review the auto-extracted values and click "Run Baseline".

6. Emissions Footprint

/footprint

What it is: Detailed forensic inventory of your total greenhouse gases.

Features: Scope 1 (boilers/diesel), Scope 2 (grid electricity), Scope 3 (freight/waste), and interactive Sankey flow chart.

How to use: Inspect emissions by stream. Click any row to view the official CEA/IPCC emission factor and audit trail.

7. Leak Points Hotspots

/leaks

What it is: Automated energy detective spotting wasteful machines.

Features: Anomaly detection (e.g. old IE1 motors, uninsulated steam pipes, air leaks) ranked by severity (Critical / High).

How to use: Plant engineers review this page before shutdowns to prioritize maintenance and equipment upgrades.

8. What-If Simulator

/scenarios

What it is: Interactive simulation sandbox to test green upgrades.

Features: Sliders for Rooftop Solar (0-100%), Boiler Fuel Switch (Coal → Biomass), Recycled Material %, and IE4 Motors + VFDs.

How to use: Drag sliders to see real-time recalculations of carbon reduction, annual ₹ savings, and payback period.

9. Circular Actions

/actions

What it is: Curated catalog of pre-costed decarbonization projects.

Features: Categorized into Quick Wins (payback < 12 mos), Cash-Positive, and Net-Cost. Shows Capex, Opex delta, and carbon eliminated.

How to use: Filter by Quick Wins and push projects directly to the Marketplace to request contractor quotes.

10. Abatement Portfolio

/portfolio

What it is: The Marginal Abatement Cost Curve (MACC) prioritization engine.

Features: Interactive MACC chart showing projects that make money (below zero line) vs projects requiring net capital.

How to use: Present this curve to CFOs and banks to prove that early quick-win profits fund larger future green upgrades.

11. Marketplace & RFQs

/marketplace

What it is: B2B clean tech directory and Request-For-Quote procurement hub.

Features: Verified solar installers, ESCO auditors, low-carbon materials catalog, and side-by-side bid comparison matrix.

How to use: Send project specs to 3 regional contractors in 1 click, compare prices and warranties, and accept the winning quote.

12. Green Logistics

/logistics

What it is: Industrial corridor freight optimization and cargo pooling engine.

Features: Multi-factory truck pooling (milk runs), modal comparison (Diesel Truck vs EV vs Train), and major export corridor routes.

How to use: Enter shipment weight and click "Join Cargo Pool" to share empty truck space, slashing freight costs and emissions.

13. Circular Network

/circular-network

What it is: Industrial symbiosis exchange: one plant's waste is another's input.

Features: Marketplace for by-products: Fly Ash, Cotton Comber Noil, Spent Solvents, Slag, and Bagasse with avoided carbon metrics.

How to use: Monetize production scraps by selling to nearby buyers or purchase secondary materials at 30% discount.

14. Compliance & CBAM

/compliance

What it is: Regulatory compliance evaluator and automated reporting tool.

Features: EU CBAM indicative tax exposure calculator, SEBI BRSR Core readiness checks, and 1-click PDF audit sheet export.

How to use: Calculate export tax liabilities before shipment and export signed audit packets for customs and banks.

15. Methodology & Emission Factors

/methodology

What it is: The transparent, open mathematical ledger and factor library powering the whole platform.

Features: Official Central Electricity Authority (CEA) grid baseline v22 factors, IPCC 2006/2019 fuel tables, and mathematical formulas for LCOA.

6. A Real-Life 30-Day Story: Rajesh Garments (Tirupur)

Day 1: The Urgent Crisis

Rajesh runs a knitwear dyeing plant in Tirupur. A major European apparel client warns him that without a certified carbon disclosure, his export goods will face severe CBAM import tax deductions at Hamburg port.

Day 3: Ingestion in 15 Minutes

Rajesh uploads his electricity bills and diesel generator logbooks using the Plant Data Intake. Local Ollama AI extracts all numbers without sharing sensitive company files. Total baseline: 2,150 $tCO_2e$/year.

Day 7: Identifying the Waste

The Leak Points page reveals his wood-fired boiler operates at only 61% thermal efficiency, and outdated IE1 motors are burning ₹11 Lakhs in excess electricity annually.

Day 12: Simulating the Fix

In the What-If Simulator, Rajesh tests 40% Rooftop Solar, Biomass Briquettes, and IE4 Motors. The model reveals: 36% carbon drop, ₹38.5 Lakhs saved annually, and full payback in 2.4 years!

Day 20: Sourcing & Logistics

He sends an RFQ to 3 verified solar installers on the Marketplace and joins a shared container truck pool on Green Logistics, saving 28% on export freight to Chennai Port.

Day 30: Certified & Compliant

Rajesh exports the official signed PDF report from the Compliance module. His European client approves the shipment with zero tax penalties and renews his supply contract for 3 years.
