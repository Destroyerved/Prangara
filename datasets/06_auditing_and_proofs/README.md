# 06 — Auditing, Provenance & Authenticity Proofs

## Purpose & Use Case
Intended for regulatory auditors, ESG rating agencies, sustainability assurance officers, and legal compliance teams. Proves that all emission factors and models are untampered, primary, and cryptographically verified.

## Included Proof Artifacts
- **`DATASET_AUTHENTICITY_PROOF.md`**: Comprehensive audit document detailing SHA-256 digital signatures, statutory references, and derivation proofs.
- **`chakra_source_verification_report.md` / `.csv`**: Factor-by-factor audit comparing legacy screening values against verified official references.
- **`source_registry.json`**: Complete metadata registry of 20 canonical sovereign and standard-setting authorities.
- **`source_checksums.json`**: 23 recorded SHA-256 hashes.
- **`download_manifest.csv`**: Download timestamps, URLs, and file sizes.
