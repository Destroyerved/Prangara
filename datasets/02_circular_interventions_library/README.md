# 02 — Circular Decarbonization Interventions Library

## Purpose & Use Case
This folder contains the 30 circular economy and energy-efficiency decarbonization interventions across 10 industrial sectors for Marginal Abatement Cost Curve (MACC) generation, capital allocation, and payback optimization.

## Key Attributes per Intervention
- **Technical Scope**: Sector, subsector, application stream (Electricity, Thermal, Materials, Waste, Water).
- **Abatement Impact**: Direct specific CO2 abatement percentage ($A_i$), target stream limit ($S_{\text{target}}$).
- **Financial Metrics**: Capital expenditure range (CAPEX in INR), operational savings (OPEX in INR/yr), simple payback period (years), abatement cost (INR/tCO2e abated).
- **Physics Bounds & Ceilings**: Maximum blend percentages (e.g. fly ash 35% ceiling per BIS IS 1489, scrap remelt ceilings), non-linearity de-rating interactions, and mutually exclusive refusal rules.

## Source Provenance
Sourced from Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) 55-cluster MSME energy audits, ADEETIE technology catalogues, and validated engineering field data.
