# 03 — Industrial Sector Benchmarks & Archetypes

## Purpose & Use Case
Provides Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) benchmarks, emissions profiles, and demo plant archetypes across 10 vital SME manufacturing sectors:
1. **Textiles & Apparel** (Surat, Tirupur, Ahmedabad)
2. **Iron & Steel Secondary Re-rolling** (Bhiwadi, Mandi Gobindgarh, Raipur)
3. **Aluminium Die-casting & Extrusion** (Coimbatore, Rajkot)
4. **Ceramics & Tiles** (Morbi)
5. **Cement Grinding & Blending** (Rajasthan, Gujarat)
6. **Pulp & Paper Packaging** (Vapi, Morbi)
7. **Glass & Glassware** (Firozabad)
8. **Foundry & Engineering Castings** (Rajkot, Coimbatore, Belgaum)
9. **Specialty Chemicals & Dyes** (Ankleshwar, Vapi)
10. **Food Processing & Agro-allied** (Mehsana, Anand)

## Transparency Disclosure
The demo plant profiles included (e.g. "Gujarat Textile Mills Ltd") have `demo_is_synthetic: true`. They are realistic archetypes designed to test the MACC recommender engine and Sankey diagrams out of the box, without exposing confidential factory tax/billing data.
